Scout recommendation card — always explains why it *may* fit, what it's based on, and what to confirm. Never "best/perfect/guaranteed".

```jsx
<ScoutResultCard
  title="Toddler Music Drop-In" provider="Astoria Arts Space" neighborhood="Astoria, Queens"
  reason="it is indoor, listed for ages 1–3, and has weekday morning drop-ins."
  confirmItems={['Current price', 'Drop-in capacity', 'Parent presence policy']}
  onViewDetails={open} providerHref={url}
/>
```
